About me

I am a Junior Fusion Energy Analyst at Fusion Advisory Services, where I provide technical information and insights to decision-makers in the fusion sector and beyond.

My work focuses on translating scientific developments into clear, useful information for those navigating the fusion industry. I aim to help bridge the gap between research and decision-making.

Research & projects

Featured · BSc Thesis

Multi-Pulse Laser Wakefield Acceleration

Used particle-in-cell simulations to compare multi-pulse and single-pulse laser wakefield acceleration. Correcting pulse spacing for relativistic electron masses, a five-pulse system achieved self-injection using 0.15 J less energy than a conventional single-pulse approach.
